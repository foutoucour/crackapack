# Retention

## Retention principles and the daily loop

The player comes back several times a day because energy recharges and the NPCs move, and every day
because quests, mini-games and the daily reward renew at midnight UTC.

1. **Short session (every 1 h 40):** I open my boosters, sell my duplicates, check my trade offers.
2. **Daily session:** I collect my 100 coins, play the mini-games, finish my 3 quests and open the
   chest.
3. **Week:** featured set, weekly challenge.
4. **Long term:** I complete binders, playsets, master sets and badges.

- **Free, forever:** no mechanic is paid for.
- **Caps everywhere:** daily gains are limited, so that 20 minutes a day is enough.
- **Nothing punitive:** no lost streak that erases progress.
- **Return after an absence:** a summary of what the NPCs did meanwhile, such as trade offers
  received.

## Daily quests and chest

Three quests a day, simple and tied to boosters and trades. Finishing all three opens a chest worth
+2 energy.

| Example quest | Reward |
| --- | --- |
| Spend 5 energy on boosters | 60 coins |
| Discover 10 new cards in your boosters | 70 coins |
| Get 2 rares or better | 90 coins |
| Sell 10 duplicates to the Counter | 50 coins |
| Complete a trade with an NPC | 70 coins |

- 3 quests drawn from the catalogue, renewed at midnight UTC.
- The reward is claimed with a click, not automatic: it is a moment of satisfaction.
- **Weekly challenge:** a longer, better-rewarded quest (e.g. "complete the commons playset of
  Guildpact").

## Collection goals and badges

Completing a collection earns coins and energy, in proportion to the effort. It is the long-term
engine.

| Milestone (per set) | Example reward |
| --- | --- |
| All commons | 650 coins + 4 energy |
| All uncommons | 700 coins + 4 energy |
| All rares | 1,200 coins + 6 energy |
| All timeshifted (Time Spiral) | To calibrate |
| Complete binder | 6,300 coins + 10 energy |
| Commons playset (4 of each) | To calibrate, above the commons binder |
| Uncommons playset | To calibrate |
| Rares playset | To calibrate, very long: big reward or rare badge |
| Master set (normal + foil + variants) | To calibrate, the biggest milestone |

- **Reversible reward:** if I sell, trade or otherwise give away the last copy that completed a
  milestone, I give the reward back and get it again when I complete it again.
- **Playsets:** the Magic-specific twist compared to Holopocket. Aiming for 4 copies gives commons a
  use, makes players keep duplicates and motivates trades with NPCs.
- **"Almost complete" section** at the top of the page: the closest milestones, to push players to
  finish.
- **Themed badges:** by block (Ravnica, Time Spiral), by guild (the 10 of Ravnica), by colour and by
  great figure (Teferi, Akroma…), earned by gathering the cards that belong to them.

## Game room: Magic mini-games

Five daily mini-games with real cards, each capped at 20 to 40 coins a day.

| Mini-game | Principle adapted to Magic | Max gain / day |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cartodle | Guess the day's mystery card; each try says what matches (colour, cost, type, set, rarity) | 20 to 40 coins depending on the number of tries |
| The Grid | A 3 × 3 grid to fill with cards that match two criteria (e.g. "Green" × "Elf") | 30 coins |
| Connections | 16 cards, 4 groups of 4 to find (same artist, same keyword…), 4 mistakes max | 30 coins |
| The Right Price | Which is worth more on TCGplayer? 10 s per duel, the gap narrows | 30 coins |
| Zoom | An illustration detail that zooms out; recognise the card as fast as possible | 30 coins |

- Counters reset at midnight UTC; the day's mystery card and grid are drawn from a seed tied to the
  date.
- One grid or mystery card a day.
- History of my scores and streaks.

## Temporary events

Locally configurable events create peaks of interest when opening boosters.

| Lever | Configurable range |
| --- | --- |
| Booster price in energy | × 0.5 to × 5 |
| Game room gains | × 1 to × 3 |
| Energy recharge | 1 energy every 5, 3, 2 or 1 min |
| Free promo packs | 1 to 200 packs |
| Duration | 15 min to 7 days |

- **Featured set of the week:** reduced cost or dedicated quests, announced the week before.
- **Out-of-catalogue set weeks:** a set outside V1 becomes openable for a week, with a dedicated
  badge.
- **Trade events:** an NPC looking for a specific card, see [Core features](core-features.md).
  Hype and crash events are not designed: they need prices that move, which the fixed Counter does
  not provide.
- **In-game announcement** (banner) with a countdown.

## Profile, showcase and NPC rankings

The profile shows my collection, and the NPCs are the point of comparison: this is what replaces
the social side.

- **Profile:** collection statistics, badges, showcase, custom binders.
- **NPC profiles** can be viewed: collection, showcase, duplicates, to prepare a trade.
- **Rankings against the NPCs:** collection value, complete binders. NPCs hold no coins, so there is
  no fortune ranking.
- **Feed of rare pulls:** mine and the NPCs' ("Ravnica_Ann opened a foil Dark Confidant").

## Notifications

Everything stays in the app: no email, no Discord.

- **Bell with a counter:** NPC trade offers, a wanted card now in the Counter's stock, milestones
  reached.
- **Return summary** on opening after an absence.
- **Browser notification** (optional): energy full.
- **Frequency:** alerts close together are grouped, and "energy full" fires once per recharge.

## Lessons from Marvel Snap

Marvel Snap does the opposite of Holopocket: no classic random boosters, a readable progression
without frustration. Several of its mechanics carry over. The analysis is based on knowledge of the
game, as the official site does not detail its progression.

| Snap mechanic | Proposed adaptation |
| --- | --- |
| Never a duplicate, cards unlocked by series ("pools") | Anti-bad-luck: jokers earned by opening boosters, exchangeable for a missing card of the same rarity (like MTG Arena wildcards) |
| Global collection level that unlocks a reward track | A collector level fed by new cards, milestones and playsets; rewards in energy, coins, badges |
| Variants and finishes as a goal of pride | Highlight rare variants (foil, showcase, borderless) in the showcase and the rare-pull feed |
| Spotlight Caches: weekly rotation announced in advance | Featured set of the week (reduced cost or dedicated quests), announced the week before |
| Themed seasons with a track | A monthly themed season (a plane, a guild) with an entirely free reward track |
| Daily and weekly missions stacked | Keep the 3 daily quests, add a longer, better-rewarded weekly challenge |
| 3-minute games | Empty your energy and sell your duplicates in under 2 minutes ("Open all", bulk sale in one click) |

To avoid: what the community holds against Snap, namely the aggressive monetisation and the new
cards that are hard to get without paying. The no-money model already rules this out.

## Lessons from MTG Arena

Arena fixes the excess of commons by converting everything above the playset of 4 into progress
toward valuable cards. Figures from 2019 to 2022, which may have changed.

| Arena mechanic | How it works | Proposed adaptation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cap of 4 | Beyond 4 copies, a card is converted automatically | Deferred: it needs a recycling mechanic, dropped for now |
| Vault | The 5th and further copies of commons and uncommons fill a gauge (~0.1% and ~0.3% per card, community values); once opened, it gives 3 uncommon jokers, 2 rares, 1 mythic | Deferred: it needs a recycling mechanic, dropped for now |
| Duplicate protection | A rare or mythic already owned in 4 copies is replaced by another card of the set; set complete: 20 gems (rare), 40 (mythic) | To dose, see below |
| Jokers in boosters | They replace a card: ~1/3 (common), 1/5 (uncommon), 1/24 (rare or mythic) | Anti-bad-luck jokers (see Marvel Snap), exchangeable for a missing card |
| Bad-luck counter | At least one rare every 15 boosters, one mythic every 30; lead: one joker every 6 boosters | A minimum joker guarantee per number of boosters opened |

**Point of tension:** Arena has no trades, so duplicate protection costs it nothing. Here, a 5th rare
is what an NPC is looking for in a trade, and full protection would dry up the supply. Two
compromises:

- Protection only for commons and uncommons. Duplicate rares stay a trade currency.
- Protection of rares only once the set is complete.

Sources: [MTG Arena Zone – Wildcards](https://mtgazone.com/wildcards), [MTG Arena Zone – Duplicate
Protection](https://mtgazone.com/duplicate-protection/), [Wizards – MTG Arena
Economy](https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/mtg-arena/mtg-arena-economy-2022-03-17).
